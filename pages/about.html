About Cotel Hocholat

Cotel Hocholat started as a single market stall selling hand-tempered chocolate bars on weekends. Today we're a small team of chocolatiers working out of one kitchen, still making everything in small batches so every bar, praline and truffle tastes the way it should.

We work directly with cocoa growers to source beans we actually like, and we don't use artificial flavouring, colouring or preservatives — just cocoa, cream, sugar, nuts and fruit. If a product looks simple, it's because we'd rather spend the effort on flavour than packaging.

Everything on this site ships worldwide in insulated, padded boxes, and every order is packed the same day it's placed.
